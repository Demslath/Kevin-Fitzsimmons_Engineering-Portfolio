  4. 2012 to 2021

    A decade of professional kitchens

    Multiple restaurants, full-time

    Ten years of line and leadership roles across a range of restaurants. These positions are omitted from my resume for length, but they are where most of the skills below were developed.

Mise en place

Every component is prepared, labelled and in place before service begins. A sampling plan or design basis follows the same principle: the critical thinking is completed before the pressure arrives.

Safety culture

Knives, open flame and a crowded line on every shift develop a habit of looking out for colleagues as a matter of routine, not of compliance alone.

Regulatory compliance

As sous chef at Double’s Late-Night, I implemented a composting program to meet municipal regulations, reduced food waste, and developed seasonal menus around local suppliers.

Team leadership

At Double’s, I led a team that had not previously worked together. Coordinating new staff through service, night after night, is management in practice, regardless of title.

Resourcefulness

At Resilience Montréal, the budget is limited but the standards are not. The same constraint applies to most municipal and environmental projects.

Composure under pressure

High-volume service with incomplete staffing builds the capacity to remain methodical. A tight deadline or a failed laboratory trial becomes a problem to solve rather than a crisis.
