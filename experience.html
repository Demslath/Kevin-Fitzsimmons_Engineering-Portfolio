Mise en place

Every component is prepared, labelled and in place before service begins. A sampling plan or design basis follows the same principle: the critical thinking is completed before the pressure arrives.

Safety culture

Knives, open flame and a crowded line on every shift develop a habit of looking out for colleagues as a matter of routine, not of compliance alone.

Regulatory compliance

As sous chef at Double’s Late-Night, I implemented a composting program to meet municipal regulations, reduced food waste, and developed seasonal menus around local suppliers.

Team leadership

At Double’s, I led a team that had not previously worked together. Coordinating new staff through service, night after night, is management in practice, regardless of title.

Resourcefulness

At Resilience Montréal, the budget is limited but the standards are not. The same constraint applies to most municipal and environmental projects.

Composure under pressure

High-volume service with incomplete staffing builds the capacity to remain methodical. A tight deadline or a failed laboratory trial becomes a problem to solve rather than a crisis.

Course projects

Course design projects

Team design projects completed alongside the capstone, each spanning analysis, design and documentation in a formal technical report.

BREE 341, Mechanics of Architected Advanced Materials, 2023

Deployable origami shelter

Problem
A deployable shelter must pack flat for transport, yet be stiff once it is deployed.
My role
Developed the crease pattern and built the paper prototypes.
Approach
A Yoshimura-type crease pattern (α = 55°, β = 128°) for a reconfigurable architected origami material that folds only along rigid creases.
Result
Paper prototypes that collapse flat and deploy in seconds, shown in the clips.

Origami mechanics · Deployable structures · Prototyping

BREE 504, Instrumentation and Controls, 2024

Salinity sensor and dilution system

Problem
Diluting a solution to a target salinity by hand is slow and difficult to repeat.
My role
Member of the design team.
Approach
A TDS sensor read by an Arduino Uno feeds a LabVIEW interface, which drives two peristaltic pumps through an NI MyDAQ and relay switches. Mounts and housing 3D printed in PLA and PETG.
Result
A working benchtop system that doses water or brine until the user-defined target is reached.

Sensors and calibration · LabVIEW · Arduino · Process control

BREE 415, Mechanics of Machines, 2024

Wind turbine gearbox

Problem
A 100 kW wind turbine rotor turns at 20 rpm, but its generator requires 1,800 rpm.
My role
Member of the design team.
Approach
Gears sized against AGMA contact and bending standards, shafts checked for fatigue (DE-Goodman) and deflection (Castigliano’s theorem), and ANSYS finite element analysis of deformation, stresses and natural frequencies.
Result
A three-stage gear train that meets every check with a safety factor of 1.5.

Machine design · AGMA standards · Fatigue analysis · ANSYS FEA

CHEE 591, Environmental Bioremediation, 2025

Remediation of the Chernobyl Exclusion Zone

Problem
Cs-137 and Sr-90 in sandy soils have carried radionuclides into four aquifers, and complete cleanup is infeasible.
My role
Member of the project team.
Approach
Characterized the contamination, then evaluated four decades of containment, topsoil removal and agricultural countermeasures.
Result
A recommendation of phytoremediation for long-term stabilization.

Bioremediation · Contaminant transport · Phytoremediation · Risk management
