  4. 2012 to 2021

    A decade of restaurant kitchens

    Many kitchens, full-time

    Ten years on the line in a range of restaurants. My resume leaves these out to stay on one page, but they’re where most of the habits below come from.

Mise en place

Everything prepped, labelled and in its place before service starts. A sampling plan or a design basis works the same way: do the thinking before the pressure arrives.

A safety culture

Knives, open flame and a crowded line, every shift. You learn to watch out for the people around you as a matter of habit, not a checklist.

Rules, and working within them

As sous chef at Double’s Late-Night I set up a composting program to meet municipal regulations, cut food waste, and built seasonal menus around local suppliers.

Leading a new team

At Double’s I ran a kitchen with a team that had never worked together. Getting new people through service, night after night, is management, whatever the job title says.

Doing more with less

Today I cook at Resilience Montréal, a day shelter. The budget is tight and the standards don’t drop, and that holds for municipal infrastructure too.

Composure

Thirty tickets on the rail and a station down. After that, a deadline or a failed lab trial is a problem to solve, not a crisis.

Education

Key classes

B.Eng. Bioresource Engineering, McGill University, 2021 to 2025. These are the classes behind the work I want to do, grouped by field. My strongest grades were in hydrology, fluids, water quality, ecological engineering and design.

Water and hydrology

  • Hydrologic Systems and Modelling BREE 509

    Simulating how water moves through watersheds and soils with computer models

  • Hydrology and Water Resources BREE 217

    Precipitation, runoff, infiltration and groundwater, and estimating flows for design

  • Fluid Mechanics BREE 305

    Pipe and open-channel flow, pressure and energy losses: the basis of hydraulic design

  • Water Quality Management BREE 533

    Where pollutants come from, how water is treated, and the standards it has to meet

  • Engineering for Land Development BREE 416

    Drainage, erosion control and water management for developed land

Remediation and waste

  • Environmental Bioremediation CHEE 591

    Using microbes and plants to break down or contain contaminants in soil and water

  • Pollution and Bioremediation NRSC 333

    How pollutants move through the environment, and biological ways to clean them up

  • Organic Waste Management BREE 322

    Composting, anaerobic digestion and treatment of organic residues

  • Bio-Based Polymers BREE 522

    Materials made from biological feedstocks, and how they are produced and break down

Ecology and restoration

  • Ecological Engineering BREE 518

    Designing with living systems, such as constructed wetlands and natural treatment

  • The Biophysical Environment ENVB 210

    How soils, climate, landforms and ecosystems shape one another

  • GIS for Natural Resource Management BREE 529

    Spatial analysis and mapping of land and water resources

  • Engineering for Sustainability BREE 420

    Life-cycle thinking and weighing the environmental cost of design choices

Design and practice
