Design and fabrication

Product design and prototyping with Fusion 360 and 3D printing

Approach

Approach

Each of these projects began with a practical problem at the workbench.

Each follows an iterative cycle: model in Fusion 360, print, evaluate, revise and print again. It is the same process as engineering design, compressed from semesters into hours. The same workflow produced the printed mounts for my capstone photobioreactor.

Bench tool

Heat-set insert press

Installing threaded inserts by hand often leaves them misaligned. This lever-arm press holds a soldering iron vertically on guide rods, so each insert is set straight and to a consistent depth. The design is documented with a complete set of assembly drawings.

Software
Fusion 360
Material
Carbon-fibre PETG
Documentation
Six assembly and part drawings
Used for
Threaded inserts in printed parts, including the capstone photobioreactor mounts

Gridfinity storage system

Custom bins for the open-source Gridfinity storage system, each shaped to a specific tool. The principle is mise en place applied to a toolbox: every item has a defined place, and missing tools are immediately apparent.
