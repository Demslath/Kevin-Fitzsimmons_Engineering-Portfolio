Things I’ve made

When the right tool doesn’t exist, I model it and print it

Why make things

Most of these started as a small annoyance at my bench.

Design it in Fusion 360, print it, find what’s wrong, change it, print it again. It’s the same loop as engineering design, just measured in hours instead of semesters. It’s also how I made the printed mounts for my capstone photobioreactor.

Bench projects

Tools for the bench

Heat-set insert press

Pressing threaded inserts in by hand leaves them crooked. This lever-arm press holds a soldering iron dead vertical on smooth rods, so every insert goes in straight and to the same depth. I documented it with a full set of assembly drawings.

Fusion 360, printed in carbon-fibre PETG

Photos and assembly drawings

Magnetic marker holders

Clip-on holders that keep a Sharpie on any steel surface, in single and double versions. Small, cheap to print, and the marker is always where I left it.

Fusion 360, printed in PETG

Photos

Gridfinity bins

Custom bins for the open-source Gridfinity storage system, each shaped around a specific tool. Mise en place for a toolbox: everything has a place, and you can see at a glance what’s missing.
